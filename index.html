GX-9000O₂ · H₂S · CO · CH₄Newer 4-gas meter with menu screen

How to use

  • Lesson: pick one at the top and follow the yellow highlights.
  • Keys: tap, or hold for a long press. Use two fingers for combos.
  • Tubes: drag from the regulator to the bag, then from the bag to GAS IN. Drag an end away to disconnect.
  • Regulator: tap the knob to open or close it.
  • ⛶ Meter view: the button on the meter shows only the meter, full screen. Tap it again to go back.

Install

Android: tap ⤓ Install at the top, or browser menu → Install app.
iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen. It works offline after the first visit.
