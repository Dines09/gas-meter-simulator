How to use this trainer

  • Choose a task to get step-by-step guidance, or Free practice to explore on your own.
  • Press the meter keys like the real ones. Hold a key for long-press. For two-key combos, use two fingers (phone) or the keyboard: ↑/Q = ▲, ↓/A = ▼, D = DISPLAY/ESC, Enter/E = POWER/ENTER. With a mouse, Shift + click latches a key down.
  • Drag tubes from the regulator outlet to the bag, and from the bag to the meter's GAS IN. Drag a tube end away to disconnect it.
  • Tap the regulator knob to open or close it, and the bag tap to open or close the bag.
